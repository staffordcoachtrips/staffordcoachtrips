❓Frequently Asked Questions

When do I pay for my trip?

Payment is only required once a trip goes on sale. Registering your interest does not reserve a seat.

Can I cancel my booking?

Cancellation terms will be shown when each trip becomes available to book.

How do I know when trips go on sale?

Register your interest and we'll contact you before tickets go on general sale.

Where are the pick-up points?

Pick-up locations are shown on each trip page before booking opens.
