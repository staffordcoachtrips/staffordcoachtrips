Frequently Asked Questions

Pick-up Point FAQs

Can I choose my pick-up point?

Yes. Where more than one boarding location is available, you can select your preferred pick-up point when booking your trip.

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before departure. This allows plenty of time for boarding.

Can I change my pick-up point?

Yes, subject to availability. Please contact Stafford Coach Trips before your travel date.

What happens if I'm late?

Unfortunately the coach cannot wait for late passengers, so please arrive in good time.
