How our pick-up routes work

Your outward journey

Each trip will have its own pick-up route based on passenger demand and the direction of travel. The final pick-up order, boarding points and departure times will be confirmed before your journey.

1

Pick-up route confirmed

The pick-up points, their operating order and departure times will be confirmed after passenger demand and the final coach route have been reviewed.

2

Arrive at your confirmed pick-up point

Please arrive at least 10 minutes before the departure time shown in your final trip confirmation.

3

Passengers board the coach

The coach will follow the confirmed route, collecting passengers from each scheduled pick-up point before continuing to the destination.

4

Return journey

The confirmed return departure time and drop-off arrangements will be provided with the final trip information before travel.

Before boarding

Important pick-up information

A little preparation helps the coach leave on time and makes the journey easier for everyone.

⏰

Arrive early

Useful answers

Pick-up point questions

How do I choose my pick-up point?

Available pick-up points will be shown for each trip. Your confirmed boarding point and departure time will appear in your final trip confirmation.

Can I board at a different pick-up point?

Please use the pick-up point shown on your booking confirmation. If you need to request a change, contact us before the trip so we can check whether it is possible.

What happens if the coach is delayed?

Road and traffic conditions can occasionally cause delays. Please remain at your selected pick-up point and keep an eye on our Facebook page or any updates sent to you.

Will the coach wait if I am late?

We cannot guarantee that the coach will be able to wait. Please arrive at least 10 minutes before the stated departure time.

Where will the coach drop us off on the return journey?

The coach will return passengers to Stafford after the trip. Return arrangements and any updates will be confirmed by the organiser or driver.

Who should I contact if I need help?

Email staffordcoachtrips@gmail.com . We aim to reply within 24 hours during our normal opening hours.
