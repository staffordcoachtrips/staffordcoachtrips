Deadlines and passenger numbers

Bookings close at 5pm, 14 days before departure. Remaining balances are due 48 hours later (5pm, 12 days before departure). Calendar dates will appear once the travel date is confirmed. All deadlines use UK local time.

A minimum of 45 paying passengers is required. Proposed capacity: 51 passengers, including free under-2s, after allowing two organiser seats on a 53-seat coach. Subject to the operator’s final seating arrangements.

If you may miss the balance deadline, contact Stafford Coach Trips for help.

Confirming your trip and paying the balance

When the date and fares are confirmed, provisional customers will receive the details directly and a balance invoice showing the deposit deducted.

You have seven days from the invoice being sent to accept or decline. Declining the date or fare means a full refund. If there is no acceptance or payment within seven days, the reservation will be cancelled, payments refunded in full and the unpaid invoice voided.

Accepting without paying does not change the separate balance deadline. Remaining balances are requested by invoice, not automatically collected. Invoices will be sent early enough to allow the full seven-day response period before the balance deadline.

Proposed cancellation and refund conditions

Draft conditions — not yet final booking terms.

Passenger cancellation before booking closure: full refund. From booking closure until the day before departure: proposed refund of 50% of the total paid. Cancellation on departure day: proposed no refund.

Cancellation deductions remain subject to applicable consumer rights and losses caused by cancellation; a larger refund may be appropriate, including where a seat is resold. Final terms must be settled before payments open.

Full refund if the trip is cancelled because the operator cancels or minimum paying passenger numbers are not reached. Full refund if you decline the confirmed date or fare within the seven-day response period.
