Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about voluntary contributions and what they mean for you.

Do I have to contribute?

No. Contributions are completely voluntary. You can use our website, book trips and travel with Stafford Coach Trips without making any contribution.

Is this a donation?

This is a voluntary contribution to support Stafford Coach Trips. Stafford Coach Trips is not a registered charity, so contributions are not charitable donations and are not eligible for Gift Aid.

How are contributions used?

Contributions may be used to support website hosting and running costs, advertising and promotion, the development of new coach trips, future website improvements and our long-term plans. Contributions are not payments for coach tickets or travel.

Will contributions help fund a community coach?

Yes. Our long-term goal is to work towards operating a dedicated community coach for Stafford. Reaching that goal will depend on many factors, and making a contribution does not guarantee that a coach will be purchased or operated by a particular date.

Will my booking be affected if I don’t contribute?

No. Your booking, travel and the service you receive will never be affected by whether or not you make a voluntary contribution.
