Useful information

Frequently asked questions

Does the £15 fare include Alton Towers admission?

No. The £15 fare is for return coach travel only. Every passenger must arrange their own admission to Alton Towers separately.

Does every passenger need a coach ticket?

Yes. One coach ticket is required for each passenger who needs a seat, including children.

How do I choose my pick-up point?

Select either Rising Brook or Stafford Railway Station during the online booking process.

Where can I find my booking confirmation?

TicketSource will send your booking confirmation to the email address used when ordering. Check your junk or spam folder if it does not appear in your inbox.

What if I need help with my booking?

Email staffordcoachtrips@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 24 hours during our normal opening hours.
